# Julia Marks Beauty

Create a luxury, feminine, modern website for a beauty brand/personal brand that blends together hair, skincare, scalp health, confidence, femininity, education, and elevated self-care. The brand should feel warm, intentional, clean, calming, high-end, and inspiring — like walking into a luxury boutique salon mixed with a wellness space.



The aesthetic should include:



Soft neutral tones (creamy whites, warm beige, muted taupe, soft black accents)

Organic textures

Minimal but emotional design

Editorial/luxury salon feel

High-end feminine branding

Clean modern fonts with elegant headings

Soft movement/animations

Inspiring lifestyle imagery mixed with beauty visuals

A balance of beauty + wellness + confidence



The overall feeling should communicate:

“Helping women feel confident, feminine, radiant, and cared for through intentional beauty.”



I want the website to feel like a luxury experience — not overly corporate, trendy, or flashy.



SITE STRUCTURE:



HOME PAGE



Large luxury hero section with emotional branding statement

CTA buttons:

Book Appointment

Shop Products

Explore Services

Include a soft scrolling section introducing:

Hair services

Confidence & self-care education

Include luxury footer with contact info and booking links

Blend motherhood, beauty, wellness, education, and self-care into the brand story

Include professional headshots and lifestyle imagery
